Data Leakage & Domain Separation

Dataset Integrity: We utilize the DataCo Supply Chain dataset (180,519 rows) strictly as historical logistics training data for delay probability estimation. It is not labeled as microchip data.

Data Leakage Prevention: The following post-transit attributes are strictly prohibited from model features:

  • Days for shipping (real)
  • Delivery Status
  • shipping date (DateOrders)

Prescriptive Decoupling: The microchip supply chain parameters (4nm chips, wafer fab capacity, assembly line budget, SLA delivery deadline) form the operational business layer fed into PuLP MILP optimization.
