SupplyPrescript Architecture

Closed-Loop Prescriptive Decision Engine

SupplyPrescript evaluates pre-transit freight risk across global multi-modal shipping corridors prior to dispatch. When disruption probability is detected, the PuLP MILP solver optimizes mitigation actions (Air Expedite, Secondary Fab Supplier, or Buffer Acceptance) subject to hard budget, capacity, and SLA bounds. Human authorizations are committed to an immutable SQLite ledger with post-delivery SLA verification.

About SupplyPrescript

Closed-Loop Prescriptive Analytics for Microchip Supply Chain Decision Support.

The Semiconductor Supply Chain Problem

Advanced semiconductor manufacturing exhibits extreme vulnerability to logistics disruption. Lead times at foundry fabs range from 20 to 50 weeks, and assembly plants run on lean Just-In-Time schedules. When in-transit microchip consignments encounter delay, downstream automotive and consumer electronics lines stop within days, incurring over $1,000,000 per shift in downtime losses.

The Prescriptive Optimization Paradigm

Traditional enterprise supply chain platforms only alert logistics managers after shipments are already delayed. SupplyPrescript closes this decision gap by integrating predictive risk inference with a mathematical optimization engine. When the XGBoost classifier estimates elevated delay risk, the PuLP Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) solver immediately computes optimal recovery alternatives (Priority Air Freight, Secondary Fab Supplier, or Delay Acceptance) satisfying hard budget, capacity, and SLA bounds.
