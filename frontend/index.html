Data Governance & Domain Decoupling Architecture

Dataset Integrity & Baseline Purpose: We utilize the DataCo Supply Chain dataset (180,519 multimodal logistics records) strictly as historical logistics training data for delay probability estimation. It is not labeled as a microchip dataset, but rather models historical freight courier transit times across geographic regions and transit modes.

Zero Data Leakage Protocol: To prevent target contamination, post-transit tracking attributes are strictly excluded from predictive model training. Only pre-dispatch scheduling and transit configuration features are provided to the XGBoost inference pipeline:

  • Days for shipping (real) — Excluded (reveals actual arrival date)
  • Delivery Status — Excluded (reveals late delivery label)
  • shipping date (DateOrders) — Excluded (reveals exact transit duration)

Prescriptive Domain Decoupling: Domain-specific semiconductor operations—such as fab wafer allocation, high-priority assembly line deadlines, component BOM quantities, and expedite budgets—are isolated within the PuLP MILP optimization engine and persistent SQLite audit trail, connecting generalized logistics intelligence to microchip manufacturing decisions.
