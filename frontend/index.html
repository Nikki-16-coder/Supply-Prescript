Data Governance & Domain Decoupling Architecture

Zero Data Leakage
Dataset Integrity & Baseline Logistics Purpose

We utilize the DataCo Supply Chain dataset (180,519 multimodal logistics records) strictly as historical logistics training data for freight delay probability estimation. It is not labeled or presented as a microchip dataset, but rather models historical freight courier transit times across geographic regions, customer segments, and transit modes.

Zero Data Leakage Protocol

To strictly prevent target contamination, post-transit tracking attributes are completely excluded from predictive model training. Only pre-dispatch scheduling and transit configuration features are provided to the XGBoost inference pipeline:

EXCLUDED Days for shipping (real) — Reveals actual arrival date
EXCLUDED Delivery Status — Directly reveals late delivery label
EXCLUDED shipping date (DateOrders) — Reveals exact realized transit duration
Prescriptive Domain Decoupling

Domain-specific semiconductor operations—such as fab wafer allocation, high-priority assembly line deadlines, component BOM quantities, and expedite budgets—are isolated within the PuLP MILP optimization engine and persistent SQLite audit trail, bridging generalized logistics intelligence to microchip manufacturing decisions without synthetic data contamination.
